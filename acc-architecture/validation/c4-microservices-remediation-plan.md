## Priority definitions and execution rule

- **MUST-FIX:** a confirmed diagram/document error or high-impact contradiction that prevents a reliable architecture interpretation. A MUST-FIX can be marked closed only after its acceptance test passes; open owner decisions remain open even when a plan exists.
- **SHOULD-FIX:** important risk or view ambiguity requiring a decision on diagram scope or a source-backed clarification.
- **OPTIONAL:** usability/traceability improvement without a C4 or architecture correctness defect.
- **EXTERNAL-REVIEW:** a policy, contract, security, legal, finance, operations, or stakeholder decision that must not be inferred. These are not technical edits a reviewer may decide unilaterally.

The ticket proposals are evidence inputs, not approved architecture. The coordinator has applied only the three narrow corrections explicitly supported by existing source material: six duplicate C1 focal-node/self-edge removals, the Integration C3 heading correction, and the Payment webhook route correction. All other proposals remain unapplied pending the approvals or scope decisions stated below.
