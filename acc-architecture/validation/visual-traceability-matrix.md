## Interpretation rules

- `COMPLETE` means the fact has a suitable visual representation and a traceable source; it does not mean implemented, tested, or owner-approved.
- `PARTIAL` means identity/visual presence exists but semantic depth, a crosswalk, or approval evidence is incomplete.
- `CONFLICTED` means authoritative sources do not currently agree; alternatives are retained and the conflict register names the required decision.
- `UNKNOWN` means the repository does not establish the fact sufficiently to draw a single definitive relationship or policy.
- C3 is required only for the seven registry-selected component-bearing containers. The 23 registry-declared C2 exceptions are represented by C2 nodes plus their replacement supporting views and are not cosmetic omissions.
