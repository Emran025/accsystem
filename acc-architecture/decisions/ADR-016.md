# ADR-016 — Multi-Seller Orders and Auditable Payment/Settlement Records

**Status:** OWNER-DIRECTION RECORDED for the multi-seller MVP direction and payment description; TECHNICAL BASELINE, OWNER-REVIEW-REQUIRED, and NOT STAKEHOLDER-APPROVED. Payment-provider capabilities and commercial policies remain CONFIGURABLE/EXTERNAL-GATE.

## Decision
A cart may contain multiple sellers and categories. ACCSystem creates a ParentOrder and seller-scoped SellerOrders. Each seller reservation is independent. The checkout saga requires all required reservations before payment authorization; a definitive failure blocks checkout. After relevant reservation and payment effects are reconciled, compensation may cover eligible known holds and eligible known financial effects, using a supported void for an uncaptured authorization or an authorized refund path for a capture. UNKNOWN or pending compensation remains open until resolved; required settlement precedes final closure. Product-visible partial orders are deferred and cannot occur implicitly.

Payment records are separate from order state and include PaymentIntent/Code, provider attempt, provider status, collection/capture, seller credit/transfer, commission payable, settlement/reconciliation link, and manually recorded payment. An append-only double-entry-style ledger or equivalent immutable posting journal is required. Every posting has a stable idempotency key, source reference, currency, amount, actor, and reversal/duplicate linkage. Manual payments require institution-scoped permission, reason, evidence reference, and approval workflow.

A provider adapter isolates capabilities such as code lookup, connected seller accounts/wallets, split payments, webhooks, reversals, and refunds. Direct seller credit, central collection followed by controlled settlement, separate provider attempts, and externally completed recorded payments remain distinct alternatives. Each is usable only when separately supported, contractually permitted, and approved by the relevant provider, Finance, Legal and institutions; otherwise the affected payment path is blocked. No provider capability is assumed.

Commission basis, rate/fixed amount, tax/shipping inclusion, settlement timing, and license-based fee meaning are policy configuration items. Software entitlement licensing is not the commercial transaction-fee contract.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Compatible aggregation can reduce buyer-facing attempts but requires compatible seller policies and provider allocation support. Separate attempts preserve policy separation and remain independently recoverable, but increase reconciliation and financial-effect cardinality. Authorize-then-capture preserves a reversible hold before collection; approved immediate capture can require refund recovery before confirmation. Direct seller credit, controlled central settlement and recorded external payment have different provider, legal, finance and institutional gates. These alternatives are retained; no one is selected without the applicable owner and capability evidence.

## Verification
Test authorization/capture modes, unknown provider outcomes, webhook replay, duplicate/manual payment prevention, ledger balancing, reversal, reconciliation, seller-credit reference matching, and tax/currency snapshot immutability.
