# ADR-003 — Shared Commerce Core for Individual and Institutional Buyers

**Decision ID:** ADR-003  
**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Date:** 2026-11-15  
**Evidence:** idea.md §3.1, §5, §8

---

## 1. Problem / Context

Two buyer personas exist: individual buyers (via ACCommerce) and institutional employees (via ACCore's internal shopping). Both need cart, order, and checkout flows. The question is whether these share the same underlying services or have separate implementations.

---

## 2. Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Description | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Shared Commerce Core** (selected) | ACCommerce and ACCore institutional buying both route through CNT-ACC-CART, CNT-ACC-ORDERS, CNT-ACC-PAYMENT | + Single implementation of complex saga logic. + Consistent order model. + Simpler maintenance. — Must handle both actor types in authorization model. |
| **B — Separate commerce stacks** | Individual and institutional buying have different codepaths | + Simpler per-persona logic. — Duplicated complex code (saga, payment, snapshot). — Divergence risk over time. |
| **C — ACCore handles institutional purchasing internally** | ACCore has its own cart/order system; ACCSystem only for individuals | + Full institution control. — ACCore becomes much larger. — ACCSystem loses visibility into institutional orders. |

---

## 3. Decision

**Both ACCommerce (individual) and ACCore institutional buying share CNT-ACC-CART, CNT-ACC-ORDERS, CNT-ACC-PAYMENT, CNT-ACC-DELIVERY.** Actor type is encoded in the JWT and enforced by CNT-ACC-IDENTITY RBAC.

**Status: PROPOSAL** — not explicitly stated in idea.md but implied by §3.1 (single Orders service) and §5 (unified identity for all actors).

---

## 4. Rationale

- CONFIRMED (idea.md §3.1): Single CNT-ACC-ORDERS service serves all buyers.
- PROPOSAL: Unified commerce core avoids duplicating the saga orchestration complexity.
- Multi-seller cart is required in MVP. ParentOrder/SellerOrders and the reservation/payment policy are defined by ADR-016; no single-seller restriction is permitted.
