# ADR-017 — Configurable Institutional Policies and External Gates

**Status:** PROPOSED BASELINE; policy owners must ratify values before production capability enablement.

ACCSystem stores versioned policy snapshots for capture timing, tax lines/rules, exchange-rate source, accounting trigger, cancellation/returns/disputes, delivery-provider priority, license plans and offline validation, support access, retention, residency, workload/SLA/RTO/RPO, and payment/commission terms. A confirmed order stores the policy/rule versions used; later policy edits never mutate the historical snapshot.

Safe defaults are fail-closed: unavailable inventory is not purchasable; license failure restricts licensed functions while preserving permitted local ERP operation; unsupported provider capability blocks the affected payment path; unknown financial outcomes remain UNKNOWN and reconcile. Finance owns accounting-trigger configuration, security/operations owns authentication/support and operational thresholds, product/finance/legal own commercial/tax/returns policies, and compliance owns residency/retention/PCI review.

External gates are not architecture questions: legal/tax determination, provider capability certification, security review, capacity/load evidence, DR exercise, and institutional contractual terms are required before corresponding production activation.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

| Alternative | Trade-off |
|---|---|
| Versioned, owner-approved platform-relevant policy snapshots (selected proposal) | Auditable historical behavior and institution-specific configuration, at the cost of policy-store availability, onboarding work and approval coordination |
| Universal hard-coded values | Simpler implementation but unsafe across institutions and difficult to audit or change without false confidence |
| Independent per-service policy settings | Local flexibility but risks inconsistent decisions, untraceable snapshots and divergent recovery behavior |

The selected proposal covers platform-relevant configuration and the references required for authorized operations. ACCore retains ownership and local enforcement of institution-internal inventory and accounting decisions; this ADR does not centralize private ERP rules.

## Verification and revisit

Planned, not executed scenarios include immutable multi-seller policy snapshots across mid-checkout changes, missing/unapproved policy, policy-store unavailability, UNKNOWN reconciliation, valid offline license/grace handling, and unauthorized support access. Reviewers must distinguish these scenarios from executed evidence. Revisit before production enablement, on policy-store design, Finance/Security/Operations/Product/Compliance review, or when provider, legal, residency, retention or accounting evidence changes.

## Confidence / Reversibility / Affected scope

Confidence is medium for the governance principle and fail-closed direction, not for any unapproved value. Reversibility is high before activation and lower after snapshots are used by orders or institutions. Affected elements include policy configuration, order snapshots, license validation, payment capture, support security and accounting hooks. Owner sign-off remains pending.
