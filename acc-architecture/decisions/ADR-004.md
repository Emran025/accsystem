# ADR-004 — ACCSystem Catalog Stores Marketplace Projection, Not Product Master

**Decision ID:** ADR-004  
**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Date:** 2026-11-15  
**Evidence:** idea.md §4.1, §4.3, §12

---

## 1. Problem / Context

ACCSystem needs product data to display listings, support search, and facilitate order checkout. The question is whether ACCSystem stores authoritative product data or a projection derived from ACCore's product master.

idea.md §4.1 establishes ACCore as the product source of truth. §12 identifies price/stock freshness as an open decision.

---

## 2. Alternatives Considered

| Alternative | Description | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Projection only** (selected) | ACCSystem stores a denormalized snapshot: title, description, price_snapshot, availability_snapshot. Source of truth stays in ACCore. | + Clear ownership. + ACCore authoritative. — Eventual consistency (staleness). — Price at order time must be re-validated. |
| **B — ACCSystem as authoritative catalog** | Institutions upload products directly to ACCSystem; ACCore syncs from there | + Single catalog. — Conflicts with ADR-001 (ACCSystem not ERP). — Institutions lose local product master. |
| **C — Live query** | ACCSystem proxies every product query to ACCore in real time | + Always fresh. — Latency. — Availability coupling (ACCSystem can't function if ACCore is down). — Impractical for search. |

---

## 3. Decision

**ACCSystem maintains a marketplace projection of products** via CMP-CAT-LISTING. The projection is updated by publication events from ACCore (via IF-ACR-PUBLISH-EVENT). The projection contains:
- Listing metadata for display (title, description, media)
- Price and availability **snapshots** (not live values)

At order checkout, CNT-ACC-OFFER re-validates price and availability in real time (triggering a synchronous call or using a short-TTL cache) before allowing the order to proceed.

**Price/stock freshness policy:** UNKNOWN (idea.md §12 — open decision). ASSUMPTION: snapshot is refreshed on each publication event from ACCore.

---

## 4. Rationale

- CONFIRMED (idea.md §4.1): ACCore owns product master.
- CONFIRMED (idea.md §4.3): ACCore publishes to ACCSystem via integration events.
- Projection pattern enables ACCSystem to serve catalog queries without depending on real-time ACCore availability.

## 5. Consequences, Risks, Mitigations

| Consequence | Type | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog may show stale prices | Data consistency risk | Re-validate at checkout via CNT-ACC-OFFER; display staleness indicator (PROPOSAL) |
| Out-of-stock items may appear available | UX risk | Same mitigation: checkout validates availability; show "verify availability" label (PROPOSAL) |
| Freshness policy undefined | UNKNOWN | Owner: OWNER_UNASSIGNED; revisit trigger: pricing/inventory freshness policy decision |
| Re-index needed after catalog reset | Operational | Re-publication procedure from all ACCore instances required |
