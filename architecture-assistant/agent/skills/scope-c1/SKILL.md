# Skill: scope-c1

Purpose: establish a testable system boundary and context.

## Procedure

Record purpose, problem, capabilities, users, ownership, technical/business boundary, exclusions, dependencies, constraints, assumptions and open questions. Build/update the diagram-selection plan before drawing and make the view choices autonomously from evidence. For a portfolio, decide whether a system-landscape view is needed, then prepare C1 context per focal system: people, directly connected external systems and important interactions. Do not show internal services as C1 entities or leak low-level technology detail into context. Validate the inventory and scope; if critical facts are unavailable, document uncertainty and continue safe work rather than pausing for routine approval.

## Required output

Provide evidence/source IDs, created/updated artifact paths, registry changes, gate result, unresolved facts and next-step recommendation.
