# Scope and assumptions policy

System boundary must name included and excluded capabilities, technical/organizational ownership, actors, external dependencies and known constraints. Tag each statement `CONFIRMED`, `ASSUMPTION`, `PROPOSAL`, or `UNRESOLVED`, with source/owner where known and impact. Ask for critical missing facts; do not fill gaps with invented behavior. External internals are opaque absent evidence. A scope change updates registry, affected mappings, diagrams and gates.
